📜 Domino Rules

Setup

Each player draws 7 tiles. Remaining tiles form the boneyard.

The player with the highest double (or highest tile) goes first.

Gameplay

Play a tile that matches an open end of the chain.

Draw from the boneyard if you have no playable tile.

Pass only when the boneyard is empty and you still cannot play.

Scoring (Tunisian)

Empty your hand → score the pip total of all opponents' remaining tiles (rounded to 5).

Blocked game → the player with the lowest remaining pip total wins and scores accordingly.

Match

First to reach the target score wins the match.
