Memory Duel Rules

  • Take turns flipping two face-down cards to find matching pairs.
  • Find a match → keep the pair and take another turn.
  • No match → cards flip back, turn passes to opponent.
  • Steal: after your opponent flips their first card, a steal window opens. Click STEAL then click the matching card to steal the pair!
  • Wrong steal → you lose your next turn.
  • Streak bonus: 3+ consecutive captures = +1 point per extra pair.
  • Steal bonus: successful steal = +1 extra point.
  • Perfect steal (first flip of game) = +3 extra points!
  • Most pairs wins. Tiebreaker: last pair captured.
