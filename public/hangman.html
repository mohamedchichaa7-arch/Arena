📜 How to Play Hangman

Guess the hidden word one letter at a time before running out of tries. The number of tries is calculated dynamically from the word's length and letter rarity — short common words are harder, long obscure words give more room to breathe.

  • Solo — pick category & difficulty, guess at your own pace.
  • PvP Race — same word, both players guess simultaneously. First to solve wins the round. Best of 5.
  • Host vs Guesser — one player picks a secret word, the other guesses while the host can taunt. Roles swap each round. Best of 6.
