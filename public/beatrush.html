How to Play

Slash the incoming blocks in the direction their arrow points, exactly as they reach the glowing hit line.

Q W E / A S D / Z X C form a 3x3 grid on your keyboard — each key points the same direction it sits in (e.g. Q = up-left, D = right, C = down-right). S (or Space) = dot notes, which can be slashed from any direction. Arrow keys also work for the 4 straight directions.

Avoid slashing bombs (dark spiked orbs). Missing a note or hitting a bomb drains your health and resets your combo multiplier.

In PvP, both players race the same note map — highest score wins.
