📜 How to Play Wordle

    
Guess the hidden 5-letter word in 6 tries. After each guess, tiles reveal color feedback:

    
    
      
  • 🟩 Green — correct letter, correct position
    • 
      
  • 🟨 Yellow — correct letter, wrong position
    • 
      
  • ⬛ Grey — letter not in the word
    • 
    

    
PvP Race: both players get the same word. Fewer guesses wins; ties broken by speed.
