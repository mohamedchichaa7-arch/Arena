Barricade Rules

  • Objective: Reach your opponent's starting row to win.
  • Turns: Each turn you may either move or place a barricade.
  • Movement: Move one cell in any direction (up/down/left/right). You can jump over your opponent if adjacent.
  • Barricades: Each player has 10. They are 2 cells wide and block passage.
  • Orientation: Barricades can be placed horizontally or vertically between cells.
  • Fair Play: You cannot completely block all paths — at least one route must remain open for both players.
  • No Overlap: Barricades cannot overlap or cross each other.
  • Strategy: Balance advancing toward the goal and slowing your opponent!
