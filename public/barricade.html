Barricade (Malefiz) Rules

  • Players: 2–4 players, each with 5 pawns.
  • Goal: Be the first to land a pawn on the goal square at the top.
  • Rolling: Roll the die, then select a pawn and move it exactly that many steps.
  • Movement: Pawns can move in any direction along the path but cannot reverse direction mid-move.
  • Barricades: White blockers that cannot be passed. Land exactly on one to capture it, then place it on any valid empty square.
  • Capturing: Land on an opponent's pawn to send it back to their house.
  • No friendly fire: You cannot land on your own pawns.
  • Exact landing: You must land exactly on the goal — no overshooting.
  • House exit: House spaces don't count against the die roll.
