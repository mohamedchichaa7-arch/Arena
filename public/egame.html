E-Game Rules

  • Players: 2 players, 5 cards each per round.
  • Cards: Emperor side: 1 Emperor + 4 Citizens. Slave side: 1 Slave + 4 Citizens.
  • Hierarchy:
    • Slave beats Emperor → 4 pts to slave
    • Emperor beats Citizen → 2 pts to emperor
    • Citizen beats Slave → 1 pt to citizen
    • Citizen vs Citizen = Draw
  • End of Round: A round ends when either player plays their special card (Emperor or Slave), OR when each player has only 1 card remaining.
  • Game: 2 rounds total — sides swap after Round 1. Highest total score wins!
  • Strategy: Playing your special card ends the round immediately. Time it wisely!
