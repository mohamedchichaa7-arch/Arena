🏰 Tower Defense — How to Play

  • Defend your lane. Enemies walk the path toward your base.
  • Buy towers (bottom bar) and place them on dark buildable tiles near the path.
  • Kill enemies to earn gold and charge your send meter.
  • When the meter hits a threshold, send a wave of enemies to an opponent.
  • Each enemy reaching your base costs 1 HP (Boss = 10). At 0 HP you're eliminated.
  • Waves are synchronized — 15s prep between each. Last base standing wins!
  • Click a placed tower to upgrade (3 levels), buy modifiers, or sell (60% refund).
  • Damage types: Arrow/Cannon/Sniper = physical (armor matters). Tesla/Inferno = magic (great vs Armored).
  • Modifiers add new abilities — e.g. Piercing, Crits, Napalm, Shatter, Cold Snap, Conductor, Wildfire, Armor Piercing, Executioner & more. Each tower can stack all three of its perks.
  • Game modes: 🏰 Classic · ⚡ Blitz (fast) · 💀 Sudden Death (3 HP) · 💰 Gold Rush (rich vs beefy) · 👑 Boss Rush (bosses every wave) · 🎲 Chaos (random twist each wave).
  • Maps: Serpentine, Switchback (open, sniper-friendly), Spiral (splash heaven), Labyrinth (tight — slows/AoE shine).
