🍀 LUCKY GUESS

📜 Chess Stratego

Orthodox chess, except you can't see your opponent's pieces, and you're allowed to lie.

Setup

  • Arrange all 16 pieces anywhere on your first two ranks (60s). Your opponent sees 16 identical tokens.
  • A pawn on your first rank can still double-step on its first move.

Moving

  • Pieces move and capture like normal chess. There is no check. You can walk into attacks.
  • Captured pieces are revealed. The capturing piece stays hidden.
  • Castling (any distance): select your King, then click a Rook on the same rank. The Rook slides next to the King and the King hops over it. Both must be unmoved, with empty squares between. Castling reveals both pieces.

Bluffing

  • Turn on Bluff Mode (B) to move any piece to any square not holding your own piece. It looks exactly like a legal move.
  • After every move, the opponent can Call Bluff until the call timer runs out or they move.
  • Caught: the bluffing piece is revealed and removed, and the move is undone.
  • False accusation: the piece is revealed and the move stands. The accused then removes one of the caller's tokens blind. If it's the King, that's a lucky guess win.

Winning

  • Capture the King. The victim gets one last chance to call bluff on the capturing move.
  • Find the King with a blind pick, or catch a bluff made by the King.
  • Resignation, timeout, or a disconnect longer than 60 seconds.
