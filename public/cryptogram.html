📜 How to Play

🔐 Cryptogram: Every letter in the quote is replaced by a unique number (1–26). The same number always means the same letter throughout the puzzle.

👆 Click any letter cell to select it, then type a letter. All cells with the same number fill simultaneously.

⌨️ Arrow keys navigate between cells. Backspace clears the current number group. Tab jumps to the next unsolved group.

💡 Hints reveal one cipher number. Use them wisely — they reduce your score!

⚔️ PvP Mode: Race your opponent! Wrong placements are rejected immediately. First to complete the full quote wins.

📅 Daily Challenge: One puzzle per day, the same for everyone. Build your streak!
